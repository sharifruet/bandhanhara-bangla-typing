**Full description** (4,000 max):
```
বাঁধনহারা বাংলা — a Bangla keyboard built around one idea: big keys mean fewer typos.

EASY BANGLA
• Tap ক for ক, hold it for খ. Every aspirate pair works this way: গ→ঘ, চ→ছ, ত→থ, দ→ধ, প→ফ, ব→ভ, স→শ, ন→ণ.
• One vowel row that changes by itself: after a consonant it shows vowel signs (া ি ু ে ো), everywhere else full vowels (অ ই উ এ ও).
• যুক্তাক্ষর made simple: ্ is always on the first screen (ক ্ ত = ক্ত), and য-ফলা and র-ফলা are single keys (ক্য, প্র).
• The most-used letters are on the first screen, sorted by how often Bangla uses them.

SMART SUGGESTIONS
• Completes words as you type (পরিস → পরিস্থিতি) and predicts the next word.
• Learns the words and phrases you use, on your phone only.

BANGLA AND ENGLISH
• Tap 🌐 to switch to a full English QWERTY keyboard with its own suggestions and automatic capitals.

EVERYTHING YOU EXPECT
• Emoji panel with recently used emoji
• Clipboard: paste what you just copied in one tap
• Voice typing button
• Drag the space bar to move the cursor. Hold ⌫ to delete whole words.
• Double-tap space to end a sentence with ।
• Light and dark themes, three keyboard heights

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The app has no internet permission. Your typing never leaves your phone.
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

How to start: open the app and follow the three setup steps.
```

**Hosting the privacy policy.** Play needs a public web address. The easiest option is to turn on GitHub Pages for the repository (Settings → Pages → branch `main`, folder `/docs`). The policy is then at `https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/privacy-policy`. A direct GitHub file link also works if the repository is public.

## App content (Policy → App content)

**Data safety.** Answer these the same way the privacy policy does:
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Is all user data encrypted in transit? Not applicable, because nothing is transmitted.
- Can users request that data be deleted? Data stays on the device. Users delete it in Settings, or by uninstalling.

Google defines "collected" as data sent off the device. This app sends nothing, and it has no internet permission to do so. That is why "No" is correct even though the keyboard learns words on the phone.
