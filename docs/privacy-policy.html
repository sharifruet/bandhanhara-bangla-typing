Privacy Policy

বাঁধনহারা বাংলা · Bandhanhara Bangla keyboard

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Bandhanhara Bangla is a keyboard for typing Bangla and English on Android. A keyboard sees everything you type, so we built this one to keep all of it on your phone.

The short version

  • The app has no internet access. It does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
  • We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting inside the app.
  • What the keyboard learns stays on your phone. You can switch learning off or delete it at any time.

What the app stores on your phone

Words it learns from your typing. To improve suggestions, the keyboard remembers words and short phrases you type or pick, with a count of how often and how recently you used them. This is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

  • It is not included in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers.
  • Nothing is learned in password fields, or in apps that ask for private (incognito) typing.
  • Words you stop using fade out automatically over a few weeks.
  • To turn learning off or erase everything, open the app, then go to Settings → Learning from your typing.

Your settings. Settings such as theme, keyboard height and language are saved on your phone. Android may include them in your device backup, like other app settings.

Recently copied text. When you copy text, the keyboard keeps it so you can paste it again from the clipboard panel.

  • It is kept in memory only. It is never written to storage, and it is forgotten after one hour or when the keyboard stops.
  • Text an app marks as sensitive, such as a password, is never kept.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your typing, learned words, clipboard or settings anywhere.
  • It does not share or sell any data.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising IDs.

Voice typing

The microphone button hands over to a voice-typing keyboard already on your phone, such as Google voice typing. That keyboard is a separate app with its own privacy policy. Bandhanhara Bangla does not receive or keep your voice.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
